Check Management - configurable office edition
Release 1.0.0-rc64

Windows Server 2022 x64 Desktop Experience application with two separate local office workspaces and named staff accounts. No real records or passwords are bundled.

Sign in as an application Administrator and open Office Information to enter your organization name, optional heading lines, workspace name, address and contact details. No DA, judicial circuit, Alabama or county heading is added automatically. Blank identity uses Check Management.

This installer is not an authorized production cutover. It creates/ensures existing compatibility groups PikeCountyWCUsers (Office 1) and EnterpriseWCUsers (Office 2), and protects each office data folder. Existing installation/data identifiers are retained for compatible upgrades. Close sessions and preserve recovery copies before any approved update.

Standard receipt/report headings are configurable. Archived original agency legal forms and signatures are not reusable organization templates and cannot be rendered in this edition. Prepare and review organization-specific forms separately.

First-run setup does not add fictional sample cases unless explicitly selected. Existing data, receipts, counters and pending reconciliation items are not changed by packaging this release.
